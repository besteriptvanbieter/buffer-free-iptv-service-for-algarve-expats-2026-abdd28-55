How to Stop Your ISP from Throttling

Some internet providers slow down streaming traffic. This can cause buffering. SubTV IP recommends two simple settings:

  1. Use Cloudflare DNS – change your DNS to 1.1.1.1. This can bypass throttling.
  2. Set buffer / cache to 5,000 ms – this gives your player more time to pre-load video data, reducing freezes.
